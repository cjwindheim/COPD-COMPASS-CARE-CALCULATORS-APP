About this tracker

BMI is calculated from weight and height. In COPD, weight and BMI can be clinically useful, but BMI does not describe muscle mass or fat-free mass. Unintentional weight loss and low fat-free mass can matter even when body weight appears stable.

This tracker is for recording and understanding measurements. It does not diagnose malnutrition, cachexia, sarcopenia, or any other condition. Discuss significant or unintentional changes with your healthcare team.

About this tracker

COPD symptoms commonly include breathlessness, cough and sputum production, and exacerbations can involve worsening dyspnea, cough, wheeze, sputum volume or purulence, and sleep disruption. This tracker records those observations without replacing validated instruments such as mMRC or CAT.

Clinical reference: GOLD 2026 Report and Pocket Guide.

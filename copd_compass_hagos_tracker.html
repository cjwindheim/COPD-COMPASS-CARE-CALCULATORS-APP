About HAGOS

HAGOS is a patient-reported outcome measure for hip and/or groin disability. It contains 37 items across six subscales: Symptoms, Pain, Activities of Daily Living, Sport/Rec, Participation in Physical Activity, and Quality of Life. The official SORC-C page states that the instrument is intended to monitor individuals and change over time.

Source: Sports Orthopedic Research Center – Copenhagen (SORC-C), Hvidovre Hospital.

Official HAGOS source at SORC-C / Hvidovre Hospital

COPD Compass Care uses its own interface and does not imply endorsement by Hvidovre Hospital or SORC-C. HAGOS is a hip/groin outcome measure and is not a COPD-specific instrument.

This tracker is for measurement and record-keeping. It does not diagnose a condition or replace professional assessment. Use the official HAGOS instructions and version applicable to your clinical or research setting.

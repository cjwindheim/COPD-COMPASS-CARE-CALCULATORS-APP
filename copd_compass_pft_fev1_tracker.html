FEV₁ percent-predicted categories shown here use the GOLD severity bands commonly used in COPD: ≥80% mild, 50–79% moderate, 30–49% severe, and <30% very severe. These bands describe airflow-limitation severity in the GOLD framework and are not a substitute for the complete PFT interpretation.

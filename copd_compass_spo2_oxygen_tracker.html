About SpO₂ Tracking

SpO₂ from a pulse oximeter is an estimate, not a direct arterial blood measurement. Readings can be affected by factors including movement, poor circulation, cold skin, nail polish, device limitations, and skin pigmentation. Look at the reading together with symptoms and trends rather than treating a single number as a diagnosis.

Follow your clinician's instructions for when to measure, what range is appropriate for you, and what action to take. Do not change prescribed oxygen or medication based solely on this tracker.
